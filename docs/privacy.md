# Privacy policy: Byte Sized AI News video uploader

Last updated: November 9, 2026

"TLDR Video Pipeline" is a personal automation tool run by the owner of the
YouTube channel Byte Sized AI News (https://www.youtube.com/@byte-sized-ai-news-11).
It makes short news videos and uploads them to that channel.

## Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and nobody else is
asked to sign in to it.

## What access it asks Google for

It asks the channel owner for permission to upload videos, set their titles,
descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and to read back the status of those
uploads on the owner's own YouTube channel.

## What data it handles

- The owner's Google sign-in token, kept in a private secret store and used only to
  upload to the owner's channel.
- The videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails it creates itself.
- Basic details of the owner's own channel and its own uploads (video IDs and
  processing status), kept so the same video is not uploaded twice.

It does not collect, store, sell or share any information about viewers, subscribers
or any other person.

## Sharing

None. Data is sent only to Google and YouTube, to perform the uploads described above.
The tool uses YouTube API Services, so YouTube's Terms of Service
(https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and Google's Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy) apply to that data.

## Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete its stored sign-in token.
After that the tool can no longer reach the channel.
